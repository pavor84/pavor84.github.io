Генератор с двумя ключевыми кадрами
Подойдёт Kling 3.0 Pro, например через Runway. В режиме Image to Video
укажите первое и последнее изображения, 10 секунд, 16:9, Audio Off.
Описание ниже рассчитано на один кадр и не требует Multi Prompt.

Если доступна только модель Runway Gen-4.5
Укажите первый кадр и 10 секунд, 16:9, 25 fps. Сверьте финальную позу
со вторым изображением после генерации.
https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/46974685288467-Creating-with-Gen-4-5

Промпт для генератора

One uninterrupted locked-off wide shot. Keep the initial camera angle,
framing and scale throughout the entire ten seconds. The cat remains
seated in the rocking chair, with the entire cat and chair visible.
The fireplace, tree, window and side table remain exactly in place.

0–2.5 seconds: the cat looks toward the viewer, smiles, then lifts the
forepaw on screen right for two small natural waves. The forepaw on
screen left holds the closed purple book still on the cat's lap.
2.5–7 seconds: the waving paw lowers back toward the book. The cat holds
the greeting pose with subtle breathing and one natural blink.
The book stays completely closed until the seven-second mark.
7–10 seconds: the cat glances down, uses the screen-right paw to open
the purple front cover around the fixed spine, and supports the book
with the screen-left paw. Both paws keep physical contact with the
book and end holding it open at a comfortable reading angle. The book
remains in the cat's lap. Retain the cat's design and the existing
gold 1С mark on the front cover wherever that surface is visible.

Keep motion slow and physically coherent. Maintain the original tabby
face, round glasses, scarf, and violet graduation cap. Only character
and book motion, plus restrained fire flicker. No dialogue, no audio,
no camera cut, no new lettering or overlays.
